title: "A Simple Mechanism-Design Approach to Informed-Principal Problems"
collection: publications
category: working_papers
extract: "A principal who privately knows her type and designs a mechanism creates a signalling problem. Her choice of mechanism can reveal her information, often generating equilibrium multiplicity depending on beliefs. Informed-principal problems are therefore often avoided in applied work. I show that, with independent one-dimensional private values and quasilinear utility (allowing nonlinear preferences over allocations), the problem reduces to standard optimal mechanism design. Using BIC--DIC equivalence, I show that every principal type attains her full-information payoff, with no signalling analysis and no equilibrium construction. The solution is a Myerson strong solution and pins down the principal’s payoff in every perfect Bayesian equilibrium. It is also robust to beliefs, on and off the equilibrium path. Truthful reporting is a dominant strategy for agents and participation is optimal ex post, meaning no assumption about how agents interpret the principal’s choice of mechanism is needed."
